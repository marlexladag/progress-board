# PROJECT NAME — Progress

One or two lines on what this board covers. Everything under a `##` is a task;
everything under a `- [ ]` is a step. Percentages are computed from the steps,
so the steps are the only thing that has to be honest.

A paragraph of context is optional. It shows under the task on the dashboard.

- [x] A step that is finished @2026-01-15
- [~] A step being worked on right now
- [ ] A step not started
- [-] A step that was dropped — it leaves the denominator entirely
